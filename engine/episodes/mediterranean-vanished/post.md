**Full script**
About 6 million years ago, the Mediterranean Sea almost disappeared. Its gateways to the Atlantic, in what is now Spain and Morocco, closed. Rivers couldn't keep up with the sun. The sea may have lost 70% of its water. What remained were shrinking, salty lakes, like giant Dead Seas. Salt piled up on the seabed, in places up to 3 kilometers thick. It's still down there. Then, 5.33 million years ago, the Atlantic broke back in at Gibraltar. One model says the flood peaked at about 100 million cubic meters per second. Roughly 500 Amazon Rivers at once. Sea level may have risen more than 10 meters a day. Up to 90% of the sea, refilled in under two years. Today, a gap just 14 kilometers wide is all that keeps it full.
